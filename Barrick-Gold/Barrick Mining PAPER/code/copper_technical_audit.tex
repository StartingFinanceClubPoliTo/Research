\section{Model and temporal validation}
American options on HG futures are inverted through a 600-step constant-volatility CRR tree and mapped to equivalent European observations. The 300-step comparison measures tree discretization, not early-exercise correctness under stochastic volatility and jumps. Four European forward models use actual matched futures and a dated Treasury NSS curve. Current 2 September calibration uses 86 observations with no reserved-strike interpolation promoted to temporal OOS.

Rolling origins are fitted independently and frozen before next-admitted-date scoring. Latent states advance over 11, 23, 9, 10, 1 and 1 calendar days. Target forward/rate conditioning makes the exercise a repricing comparison. Equal-date $R^2=1-\sum_t MSE_{m,t}/\sum_t MSE_{b,t}$ differs from pooled scoring. The published RMSE is mean daily RMSE. All four models share 480 targets; observed-IV persistence has 375 supported targets inside its origin interpolation hull.

\section{Measure audit: gold and copper}
Option calibration is under $\mathbb Q$ using deterministic rates. The corporate simulations instead define an artificial law $\mathcal R$, using transferred coefficients, independent WACC shocks and assumed mean schedules. No Radon--Nikodym density, physical price/variance premium or jump-intensity/mark-law tilt is estimated. No $\mathbb Q\to\mathbb P$ change is performed. Reanchoring GLD to gold does not retain a traded gold pricing law. Similarly, interpolating copper futures across delivery dates does not specify a fixed-delivery future's time drift.

Under the deterministic-rate pricing convention a fixed-delivery future has zero drift, while interest discounts option premia. The scenario schedule's nonzero drift is used only for an imposed mean path. Numerical tests with nonzero jump moments confirm unit expected innovations under the frozen-step compensated simulation for GBM, Heston, Bates and Hawkes. The compensator is $\lambda(\mathbb{E}[e^J]-1)$, using the simulated mark law; the Hawkes step uses its left intensity. These tests verify implementation, not a physical measure. WACC is not a short rate or a pricing kernel. A risk-free substitution alone does not repair the corporate claim. The CLI rejects unimplemented physical or corporate risk-neutral valuation laws and exports this boundary in the run manifest. All 22 Paper tests pass; the measure-audit replay reproduces the previous summary and quantile tables exactly.

\section{Permitted reading and replay}
The extension fills copper's operating omission. It does not estimate mine expansion, reserves, closure obligations, corporate FCFF, debt allocation, economic benefit sharing or diluted shares. An attributable flow must not receive another deduction for the same partners' interests. Consolidated financial claims require restatement before an equity bridge. The inherited gold vector still moves from company actuals to an eight-mine forecast; this scope break is disclosed.

The public Paper entry point supports \texttt{python main.py --copper --output-dir <new-directory>}. It reads the frozen derived copper bundle and the audited 7 September gold configuration, checks evidence hashes and refuses to overwrite an existing result. Raw-market reacquisition and historical calibration are documented in the companion UNIFICATO code. Offline replay establishes computational reproducibility of the frozen scenario, not independent verification of licensed input quotes.
